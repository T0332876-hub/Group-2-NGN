## Purpose of This Research

The purpose of this research is to analyse existing charity and community
network websites to identify best practices, common features, and design
approaches that can inform the development of the Nottingham Growing
Network (NGN) website. By reviewing similar organisations, the team can
ensure the NGN website meets user expectations and delivers genuine value
to both the public and the NGN trustees.

## Overall Conclusions & Recommendations for NGN

Garden Organic is the best overall website because it has a professional
design, clear structure, and useful features such as events, newsletters,
and a members area. For NGN it is better to combine this approach with
the network model used by Transition Network, where multiple local groups
are connected through one central platform.

Across all five websites, common features identified as important include:
- Clear navigation
- Events pages
- Strong community-focused content
- Interactive map showing locations

NGN should include both a public-facing platform and a members area to
support communication between trustees and the wider community. The
website should be simple and easy to use to avoid the complexity issues
seen in some of the analysed sites.

The most important feature NGN should include is an interactive map
showing all 35 community gardens in Nottingham, allowing users to easily
find locations, contact details and activities.
